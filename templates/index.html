📖 Reading the Scanner
Score

A composite 0-100 number combining all the columns below. Higher = more scalp-worthy. The formula weights Volume Ratio (30%), ATR% (30%), |Gap%| (20%), and Range% (20%).

60+ Hot — high activity 35-59 Moderate <35 Low activity
Chg% — Change Percent

How much the price moved from yesterday's close to right now. Shows current momentum direction. Green = up, Red = down. Large Chg% means the stock is moving today.

Gap% — Overnight Gap

The difference between today's open and yesterday's close. A stock that closed at $100 and opened at $103 has a +3% gap. Big gaps usually mean news, earnings, or a catalyst happened overnight. Scalpers love gap stocks because they trend and have high volume at open.

  • ±1-2% — Normal, mild interest
  • ±2-5% — Notable catalyst, expect strong volume
  • ±5%+ — Major event (earnings, FDA, etc.)
Vol Ratio — Volume Ratio

Today's volume divided by the 20-day average volume. This is arguably the most important column for scalping. It tells you if more people than usual are trading this stock right now.

  • 1-1.5x — Normal day
  • 1.5-3x — Above average interest
  • 3-5x — Unusual volume, something is happening
  • 5x+ — Massive volume spike — news, earnings, or short squeeze

The bar next to the number is a visual indicator (fills up to 5x).

ATR% — Average True Range

The stock's average daily price swing over the past 14 days, expressed as a percentage of the price. This tells you how much a stock typically moves in a single day.

Example: A $50 stock with ATR% = 4% typically swings $2/day. That's your profit potential per trade.

  • <1% — Barely moves. Skip for scalping.
  • 1-2% — Moderate, fine for large positions
  • 2-4% — Good scalp range
  • 4%+ — Very volatile, bigger moves but riskier
Range% — Intraday Range

How much the stock has moved today specifically: (Day High - Day Low) / Open × 100. Compare this to ATR% — if Range% is already near ATR%, the stock may have used up most of its daily move. If Range% is low but ATR% is high, there may still be a move coming.
